How To Create A Good Story

I've always liked creating stories. In my experience, I've written mostly fantasy and absurd fiction. But either way, whether it's a comedy movie script or a murder mystery book, every story must have a couple key things. So here I have a couple of introductory tips for:

  1. Writing good characters: Creating protagonists, villains, and side characters that have interesting characteristics.

  2. Building an evolving plot: Making sure the reader is always engaged with what's going on.

  3. Eventually ending it all: Every good story needs a good ending.

Enjoy!
